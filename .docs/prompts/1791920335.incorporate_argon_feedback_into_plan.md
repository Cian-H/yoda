## Reasoning

Gemini 4 Argon reviewed Yoda's implementation across `belnap.py`, `belnap_transformer.py`, `encoders.py`, `engine.py`, `ltn.py`, and `losses.py`. Argon diagnosed the root causes behind two major experimental failure modes:
1. The 36.5% plateau in 95% of runs (catastrophic gradient vanishing in Epoch 1 caused by Jacobian minimization in the LTN decisiveness penalty, unmasked `[PAD]` token flooding and sequence-length saturation in sigmoid attention, and exponential gradient decay through convex residual joins with unnormalized sigmoids).

To turn this feedback into actionable engineering progress, the plan must decompose these mathematical findings into prioritized, clearly explained phases and individually-addressable work packages in `.docs/todos/` and a comprehensive roadmap in `.docs/plan.md`.
