# Request

Switch learning rate scheduling to 5% warmup followed by 95% cosine annealing, eliminating the excessive 30% OneCycleLR ramp, and clarify softmax elimination in parallel candidate evaluation.

## Reasoning

A 30% warmup (`pct_start=0.30`) was designed for CNN training with SGD+momentum. For AdamW on frozen backbones with neural-symbolic transformer reasoning layers, spending nearly a third of the epoch budget climbing to peak LR starves the network of productive training steps. Standard practice for transformers is 3–5% warmup (stabilizing AdamW's running variance estimator $\beta_2$) followed by cosine decay for the remaining 95% of steps.
