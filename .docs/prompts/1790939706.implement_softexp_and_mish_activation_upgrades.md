## Implementation
- Created `src/yoda/architecture/activations.py` containing `SoftExp` with modular methods (`_linear_taylor`, `_logarithmic_branch`, `_exponential_branch`) and numerical safety guards against `NaN` and `Inf`.
- Re-exported `SoftExp` in `src/yoda/architecture/__init__.py` and `src/yoda/__init__.py`.
- Wrote full unit test suite `tests/test_activations.py` confirming alive gradients at $\alpha=0.0$, vectorization, numerical safety, and branch continuity.
- Updated `BelnapMultiheadPooledAttention` in `src/yoda/architecture/attention.py` to integrate `SoftExp` on continuous input adapter and re-centered `init_bias` to `0.0`.
- Updated `BelnapAttention` in `src/yoda/architecture/belnap_transformer.py` with smooth knowledge gating ($0.5 + 0.5 \cdot k_{\text{src}}$) and default `init_bias=0.0`.
- Updated `BelnapFFN` to use smooth `F.mish` activations on hidden expansions.
- Decoupled `BelnapDecisionHead` decision ranking logits from the $k \to 0$ collapse trap.
- Documented architectural decisions in ADR-0006 (`0006-softexp-and-mish-activation-architecture.md`) and updated ADR index.
